UC SAN DIEGO · M.S. THESIS · IN PROGRESS

Motorized 3D Ultrasound Imaging

A motorized linear scan platform for repeatable musculoskeletal ultrasound imaging, initially focused on the Achilles tendon.

Problem

Handheld scans depend on probe motion and positioning. This thesis explores controlled linear motion and position synchronized with sequential 2D ultrasound frames as a route toward 3D tissue representation.

My work

I configured and operated the Oriental Motor EZ Limo rail and designed a probe mount and support frame for the Clarius L15 HD3 handheld probe. The frame is being sized around the tissue phantom and spatial and temporal calibration setups.

Current stage

The rail is moving, the probe mount has been assembled on the carriage, and the frame CAD is being refined. Image acquisition synchronization, calibration, reconstruction, and quantitative validation are planned work, not completed results.
